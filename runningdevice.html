About the Minor

The Visual Design & Front-End Development Minor is a 30 ECTS programme built around self-directed learning. Rather than following a fixed curriculum, students define their own focus, set individual learning objectives, and determine their own deliverables across a series of five-week sprints.

This open structure gave me the opportunity to shape the minor around the areas of visual design I wanted to develop further. I chose to focus mainly on 3D animation, motion graphics, and projection mapping, while also exploring front-end development.

Through experimentation and learning by doing, I gradually developed my skills using Blender, After Effects, and Resolume. What interested me most was exploring how digital visuals could move beyond the screen and become part of physical spaces, which eventually led me towards projection mapping and live visual experiences.

Sprint 1

Foundations in Motion & 3D

The first sprint focused on building a foundation in both After Effects and Blender.

In After Effects, I experimented with psychedelic motion graphics, exploring color, rhythm, looping animations, and visual intensity.

In parallel, I started learning Blender from scratch, focusing on basic 3D concepts such as modeling, lighting, materials, and simple animation.

Sprint 2

Blender Exploration & Projection Mapping

In the second sprint, I went deeper into Blender, increasing the complexity of my 3D animations and experimenting with more organic forms.

At the same time, I started learning Resolume, a software widely used for projection mapping and VJ performances.

The goal of this sprint was to understand how digital animations behave when projected onto real-world surfaces, using a beamer and a physical projection setup.
